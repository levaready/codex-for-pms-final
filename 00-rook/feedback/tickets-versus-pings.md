# Support tickets versus ping records

## Finding

The sources agree strongly on the symptoms and timing, but do not prove their cause. Reviewed all 147 tickets (29 June–7 September) against all 1,696 pings (29 June–6 September). Ticket filing date is not incident date.

Daily misses jump on 12 August, the release date. The first new disappearing-offer ticket is filed that day (#3041, The Drift). Captain Vantage's #3043 is filed 13 August about the preceding evening. First quiet-stretch complaint: Farlight #3060 on 17 August, five days after release, describing roughly three days of silence. Reduced opportunities become clear in the week starting 17 August; missed offers precede the sharp weekly volume decline for Farlight and Undertow.

This does not imply problems never happened earlier: #3014 (14 July) describes Undertow missing a ping while out of signal; #3034 (4 August) describes late/missing notifications on Ironvale's older phone. The narrow disappearing-offer classification below includes #3014 but excludes duplicate notifications and general delivery complaints such as #3034.

## Do individual claims match?

- All 15 explicit seven-day counts filed through 6 September match a rolling 168-hour count ending at filing time. These include Farlight #3071 (4), Undertow #3073 (6), Ashgrove #3083 (6), Halfmoon #3086 (8).
- Two more explicit counts on 7 September (#3145, Undertow, 1; #3147, Halfmoon, 8) match the available rows but cannot be fully verified because ping data stops on 6 September.
- All 16 disappearing/missed-offer tickets have at least one missed ping for the same responder on the described calendar date. Some dates have multiple misses, so this is compatibility, not a unique ID match. Relative dates were manually resolved from ticket text using local calendar dates.
- #3041, filed 12 August, says The Drift lost an offer that morning: #40982 at 11:41:57 is missed.
- #3043, filed 13 August, describes Captain Vantage the previous evening: #40987 at 19:03:31 on 12 August is missed.
- #3110, filed 29 August, describes Farlight the previous night: #41245 on 28 August 19:28:20 is missed.
- #3140, filed 5 September, describes Undertow that afternoon: #41394 at 15:17:47 is missed.
- “Days of silence” are rounded recollections, not exact elapsed time. #3060 filed 17 August 11:03 says three days; last recorded Farlight ping was 14 August 21:22, about 2 days 14 hours earlier. This is broadly consistent, not precisely three 24-hour periods.
- A sent ping is not proof of a visible notification. Missed status supports the reported failure to accept, not the claimed on-screen duration.
- Quiet-stretch tickets concern Farlight, Undertow, Corporal Ashgrove and Halfmoon. Vesper and Meteor Mite also lose volume, but have no tickets in this extract. Absence of a ticket is not absence of harm.
- Some disappearing-offer reporters gain volume (e.g., Captain Vantage, Nightwell). Disappearing offers and starvation overlap but are not identical problems.

## Queries

```sql
SELECT * FROM rook.support_tickets ORDER BY filed_at, ticket_number
```

```sql
SELECT sent_at::date AS day, COUNT(*) AS pings, COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE outcome='taken') AS taken, COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE outcome='missed') AS missed, COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE outcome='turned down') AS declined FROM rook.pings GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 1
```

```sql
SELECT date_trunc('week',sent_at)::date AS week, responder, COUNT(*) AS pings, COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE outcome='taken') AS taken, COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE outcome='missed') AS missed FROM rook.pings GROUP BY 1,2 ORDER BY 1,2
```
