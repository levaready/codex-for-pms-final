## Interpretation and limits

All 16 responders, all 15 incident areas and all four time bands declined; 18 of 20 derived incident actions declined. Farlight, Meteor Mite, The Undertow and Vesper have the steepest responder declines. This cohort was selected after observing outcomes, so it is exploratory. Their weekly offers fell from 43 in release week to 16, 6 and 3; aggregate recovery can reflect changing offer mix. Raw period totals cover unequal durations. No adjustment for responder, area, hour or incident mix was applied; these dimensions overlap and do not establish independent causes. Small post-release responder and action samples make precise rankings uncertain.

Incident action is derived from free-text descriptions by removing the subject and has/have/is/are prefix. It is not an official severity, capability or incident taxonomy. Area refers to incident area, not live responder location. No response timestamps or location telemetry are exposed. Release-day classification uses midnight because deployment time is unknown. Source: Rook database pings joined to callouts by callout_number.
