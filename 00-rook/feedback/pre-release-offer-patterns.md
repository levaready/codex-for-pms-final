# Before-release misses, declines and next-week offers

Source: fresh Rook database query. Six complete pre-release weeks, 29 June–9 August 2026; 16 responders, 96 responder-weeks. Compare 80 consecutive week pairs; exclude transitions into the mixed release week. Descriptive, not causal: no controls for demand, availability, area, score or capability. Repeated observations within responders are not independent.

| Prior week | Pairs | Fewer next week | Same | More | Mean change in offers |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| At least one miss | 22 | 8 | 2 | 12 | +0.27 |
| No misses | 58 | 23 | 18 | 17 | -0.10 |
| 3+ declines | 31 | 14 | 5 | 12 | -0.06 |
| 0–2 declines | 49 | 17 | 15 | 17 | +0.04 |

Decline threshold is an exploratory split, not a known product threshold. Counts depend on opportunity volume, so these groups are not matched. All next-week offer changes lie between -2 and +2. No clear aggregate evidence of pre-release offer starvation after misses; the decline split shows a small unadjusted difference in frequency of decreases, not proof of a causal effect. Weekly aggregation can hide individual ranking changes or within-week effects.

## Query

```sql
WITH w AS (SELECT date_trunc('week',sent_at)::date AS week, responder, COUNT(*) AS pings, COUNT(*) FILTER(WHERE outcome='missed') AS missed, COUNT(*) FILTER(WHERE outcome='turned down') AS declined FROM rook.pings WHERE sent_at >= TIMESTAMP '2026-06-29' AND sent_at < TIMESTAMP '2026-08-10' GROUP BY 1,2) SELECT *, LEAD(pings) OVER(PARTITION BY responder ORDER BY week) AS next_week_pings FROM w ORDER BY responder,week
```
