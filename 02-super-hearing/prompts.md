# 02 · Super Hearing: prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook twelve days ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly. Last session you built a context file and met the
company.

You still do not know what actually went wrong.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Codex saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below, not the starter prompt, updates
AGENTS.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.

what additional information can we determine about the offers disappearing before the responders can accept?

### 2.

what would be the next question s that should be asked to answer *Based on the interviews, the trouble with 4.2 is&#xA0;*

### 3.

### 4.

**When did you first notice this? Can you identify examples before and after the August release?**

### 5.

what would be a great follow up questions to the data that was just received

### 6.

For responders reporting quiet stretches, did their missed offers increase first—and did their offer frequency fall afterward?

### 7.

### 8.

based off all the questions asked so far what conclusion can be drawn

### 9.

what pattern can be determined based from both the interviews and tickets

### 10.
